Welcome to Webbed!

What is Webbed?

Webbed is a way to combine all your online feeds as an alternative to the many feeds you may be interested in scattered across the web.

Many creators offer their content on several sites, some of which they might profit more from (like creator-owned platforms). Keeping track of new content across platforms, especially when having to take factors like these into account, can be cumbersome. That's where Webbed comes in. Get your favorite Dropout shows, game dev blogs, Twitch streamers and YouTube creators and combine all their posts into one feed.

How does it work?

Using Real Simple Syndication (RSS), Webbed can fetch feeds available on your favorite websites. RSS lets you subscribe to updates from websites, blogs, news sources, and even social media platforms.

Many sites offer RSS feeds, usually marked with an orange icon. By using RSS, you take control of your content consumption, getting updates directly without relying on algorithms. This means you won't miss important content and can reduce your dependence on social media and search engines. Webbed functions as an 'RSS aggregator' here, which combines different RSS feeds to display them as one. It has some convenient features like built-in support for YouTube, Dropout, Nebula, Twitch, and Bluesky. That means that you won't have to interact with RSS at all for these sources! If you want to add different sources, that is still possible, as long as you provide the RSS feed to them. Information on how to add feeds and find RSS feeds in a website is provided here. If you're already using another RSS aggregator or looking to move to a different one, you can export or import a standardized OPML file.

Getting Started

Ready to jump in? Add your first feeds and start bundling your content sources!

Adding Your First Feed

You can add feeds using the 'Add Feed' button in the sidebar. When opening this menu, you can choose between different sources that have built-in support. For sources with built-in support (YouTube, Dropout, Nebula, Twitch and Bluesky), you can simply paste a regular URL to the account you want to follow. Besides that, some sources have additional options (e.g. Include Shorts (YouTube) and Nebula Plus Only (Nebula)). In Dropout.tv's case, there is a dropdown of all the available shows. Additionally, you can choose to follow a YouTube playlist instead of an entire channel by using the URL to that playlist or follow a Nebula category instead of a singular channel!

Example URLs
YouTube channel: https://www.youtube.com/@...
YouTube playlist: https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=...
Nebula channel: https://nebula.tv/...
Nebula category: https://nebula.tv/explore/videos?category=...
Bluesky channel: https://bsky.app/profile/...

Adding Feeds From Other Sources

If your feed is not included as a built-in source, you are likely still able to add it using the 'Other' category. This is only necessary if your feed source does not have built-in support in Webbed! To do this, you need to provide the URL to the RSS feed. Sometimes adding '/feed' to a website's URL already provides this. However, sometimes it is more hidden. Usually, browser extensions like RSS Launchpad (Firefox) and Get RSS Feed URL (Chrome).

Editing and Deleting Feeds

To edit the display name of a feed or move it to another folder, you can press the 'kebab' button (three vertical dots) next to the feed you want to edit. In the menu that opens, you can also change source-specific settings, like for YouTube and Nebula, and copy the RSS URL, if you need it. After you edit a feed or its name and press 'Save', this change will be visible in the item feed and the sidebar menu. If you would like to delete a feed, simply press the red 'Delete' button.

Feed and Folder Views

If you're interested in viewing what content is available from one specific feed, you can click on the name of that feed - either under an item or in the sidebar - to view only items from this feed. If you would like to view a subset of feeds, add them to a folder and click on the folder name!

Organizing Feeds

On how to add and edit folders.

If you're adding a lot of feeds, some structure might be nice. That's why you can also create folders to organize your feeds. Click the 'Add Folder' button and name your folder. To add feeds, select this folder name when editing or creating your feed. To edit your folder's name, press the 'kebab' button (three vertical dots).

Syncing Devices and Migrating Between Aggregators

Use OPML to manually sync feed lists across devices and migrate from and to Webbed.

That means you should use the 'Import OPML' and 'Export OPML' buttons. OPML is a file format that can include a structured overview of your feed organization, including custom feed names and folder structure. In the sidebar, at the bottom, you will find these two buttons. This is especially useful to quickly sync your feed lists between your phone and desktop, for example. However, it is also useful to import your feeds from a different RSS aggregator, or export them as a backup or to migrate to an alternative RSS aggregator.
